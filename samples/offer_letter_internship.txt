INTERNSHIP OFFER LETTER

Date: 29 June 2026

To the Intern,

We are pleased to offer you an internship in our organization. The details of the
internship are below.

Position: Front-end Intern
Start Date: 3 July 2025
End Date: 28 April 2026
Stipend: INR 3,000 per month and up to 15,000 INR incentives which will vary
according to your performance.

1. Your first 10 days of the training period shall be an unpaid training period.
The work schedule for this position is 6 days a week from 11.00 a.m. to 8.00 p.m.
As a temporary employee in the role of an intern you will not be eligible for any
company-sponsored benefits.

2. This is a target based job and your performance will be evaluated based on the
target achievement.

3. If at any time you wish to discontinue the trainee period you will have to serve
a notice period of 1 month or pay a compensation equal to 1 month stipend. If your
performance is not satisfactory, the company has the right to terminate this
internship at any time.

4. You will receive your full and final compensation post resigning from the company
after 45 days only after completion of all the exit formalities (T&C applicable as
per the discussion during exit).

5. The original documents you provided will be returned to you once the training
period is completed. Anytime you wish to take back the original documents during
your training period, you are required to submit a valid replacement document which
is approved by HR.

6. All the work that you produce at or in relation to the Company will be the
intellectual property of the Company. You are expected to refrain from talking about
your work in public domains (both online such as blogging and social networking)
without prior discussion and approval with your mentor.

7. You are being hired as an intern and any future requirements on the technical
side, including but not limited to AI/ML, mobile app development and DevOps, shall
be assigned as directed by your mentor. Terms and conditions may be amended from
time to time.

8. You will be working remotely until further notice. A return to office date will
be decided at a future point in time. You are expected to hand over all work or data
stored on your personal computer at completion.

9. Upon completion of the internship the organization will evaluate your performance
and may offer you a full-time position, based on vacancies.
