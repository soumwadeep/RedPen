INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Bright Harbour Studio Ltd ("Client") and the
contractor named in the Statement of Work ("Contractor").

1. SERVICES AND INDEPENDENCE

Contractor shall provide the services described in the Statement of Work
attached as Schedule A. Contractor determines the method, hours and location of
its work, is not subject to Client's internal policies, and may provide
services to other clients provided it discloses any direct competitor named in
writing for this project.

2. FEES, DEPOSIT AND PAYMENT

Client shall pay the fixed fee stated in Schedule A. A deposit of 40% of the
fee is due on signature and before Contractor starts, and the balance is due
within 14 days of the invoice date. Payment is not contingent on Client's
receipt of funds from any third party. Invoices unpaid after 14 days accrue
interest at 1.5% per month, and Contractor may suspend work on 7 days' written
notice while an invoice remains overdue. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes,
which Client pays in addition where it is required to collect or withhold them.
Reasonable project expenses over 50 USD approved in writing in advance are
reimbursed on production of receipts.

3. SCOPE, REVISIONS AND CHANGES

Schedule A lists each Deliverable with objective acceptance criteria. The fee
includes 2 rounds of consolidated revisions per Deliverable. Either party may
raise a written change request stating the effect on fee and schedule; a change
takes effect on written acceptance, and work ordered before acceptance is
charged at the hourly rate in Schedule B. Deliverables not rejected in writing
within 10 business days with stated reasons are deemed accepted and the invoice
falls due.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Title to each Deliverable passes to Client upon Client's receipt of payment in
full for that Deliverable. Until then Client holds a licence to use the
Deliverable for internal review. Contractor retains all right, title and
interest in its pre-existing materials, templates, libraries and tooling
("Background IP") and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, irrevocable
licence to use Background IP as embedded in the Deliverables. Contractor's
tools, source and working files are not Deliverables and may be provided as a
separate paid service at the rates in Schedule B. Contractor may show
reduced-resolution excerpts and the Client name in its portfolio, website and
award entries after public launch. Contractor waives moral rights only to the
extent necessary for Client to use the Deliverables as intended, and retains
the right to be credited.

5. LIABILITY

Each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid
or payable in the 12 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for
indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive loss, or for lost
profits or revenue. Contractor indemnifies Client against third-party claims
that the Deliverables infringe IP rights, provided Client gives prompt written
notice and Contractor controls the defence, and excluding claims arising from
Client's modifications, materials or use. Contractor maintains professional
liability insurance with limits of at least the fees paid under this Agreement.

6. DELIVERY AND DELAY

Dates in Schedule A are agreed in good faith. Where Contractor anticipates a
delay it shall notify Client promptly and the parties shall agree revised dates.
Client's sole remedy for delay attributable to Contractor is to cancel the
affected milestone and pay for work performed to that date.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice. On any
termination Client pays for all work performed to the termination date and for
non-cancellable commitments already made, and retains Deliverables only on
payment. Renewal requires both parties' written agreement and rates are reviewed
at each renewal.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

Confidential Information means information disclosed in writing and marked
confidential, or information a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential. It excludes general skills, knowledge and experience retained by
Contractor, information that becomes public without breach, and information
already lawfully held. Obligations continue for 5 years after termination, and
indefinitely only for information that is a genuine trade secret.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, not
to be unreasonably withheld. Disputes are first escalated to senior
representatives of each party for 14 days, and failing resolution are decided by
the courts of the jurisdiction in which Contractor is established, or by
small-claims procedure where the sum in dispute is below its threshold. Either
party may seek interim relief where a breach occurs.

10. GENERAL

This Agreement, together with its Schedules, is the entire agreement. Each party
acts reasonably and in good faith in exercising any discretion given to it
here. If any provision is unenforceable the remainder continues in effect.
