NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is made between the Company and the Supplier.

1. Definitions. "Confidential Information" shall include all information or data,
whether electronic, written or oral, relating to the Company's business, operations,
financials, services, facilities, customers and contact details, which comes to the
knowledge of the Supplier in connection with the Purpose.

2. Obligations. The Supplier shall keep confidential all Confidential Information and
shall not disclose it to any third party. Upon discovery of any prohibited use or
disclosure, the Supplier shall immediately notify the Company in writing. The
obligation contained above shall survive any termination or expiration of the
Agreement.

3. Limitations. This Agreement shall not restrict disclosure of information that the
Supplier can evidence through sufficient documentation was, at the time of receipt,
otherwise available.

4. Return of Materials. Upon completion of the business objective or the termination
of this Agreement, and upon the written request of the Company, an authorized officer
of the Supplier shall promptly, at the option of the Company, either return to or
destroy all Confidential Information.

5. Staff. The Supplier's staff assigned to perform services in connection with the
Purpose will comply with applicable confidentiality policies, and the Supplier shall
be responsible for any breach by its staff.

6. No License. No license or conveyance of any rights held by the Company under any
discoveries, inventions, patents, trade secrets or copyrights is granted or implied by
this Agreement.

7. No Publicity. The Supplier shall not promote or otherwise disclose the existence of
the relationship between the Parties for purposes of soliciting or procuring sales,
clients, investors or other business engagements.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of
India. Any dispute shall be referred to senior representatives of both Parties for
negotiation and, failing resolution, shall be resolved through Arbitration in India.
The venue of arbitration shall be [New Delhi or Mumbai].

9. Injunctive Relief and Damages. The Parties acknowledge that the Company's remedies
at law may be inadequate and that the Company may suffer irreparable harm in the event
of breach. Accordingly the Company shall be entitled to seek preliminary and final
injunctive relief without a requirement to post bond, as well as any and all other
applicable remedies at law or in equity, including the recovery of damages.
Additionally, the Supplier agrees to keep indemnified against any losses or damages
(including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising due to the breach of this Agreement by
the Supplier.

10. Waivers; Assignment. Neither this Agreement nor the obligations of the Supplier
shall be assignable or transferable by the Supplier without the prior written consent
of the Company.

11. Term. The obligations of each receiving party hereunder shall survive until such
time as all Confidential Information of the other party disclosed hereunder becomes
publicly known and made generally available through no action or inaction of the
receiving party.
