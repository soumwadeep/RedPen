SERVICE AGREEMENT

Between Lumen Foods Co. ("Client") and the designer named on the invoice
("Designer").

1. SCOPE OF WORK

Designer shall produce a brand identity package: logo suite, colour palette,
typography specification and a 12 page brand guide, as described in the email
thread attached to this agreement. Designer shall make such further changes to
the package as Client may reasonably request from time to time until Client is
satisfied with the result.

2. FEES

The fixed fee for the package is 2,400 EUR. Client shall pay the fee within 45
days of receiving a valid invoice. Client shall pay a further 30% of the fee for
each additional package requested. Fees are inclusive of all applicable taxes.

3. COPYRIGHT AND USE

All intellectual property in the Deliverables, including sketches, drafts and
source files, vests in the Client upon creation and Designer assigns all such
rights to Client. Designer shall not use the Deliverables or reference Client as
a client in any portfolio, website or social media without Client's prior written
approval, which may be withheld.

4. TIMETABLE

The package is to be delivered by 30 November. Time is of the essence. Designer
shall keep Client updated on progress.

5. TERMINATION

Client may terminate this agreement at any time by written notice. On
termination Client shall pay for approved work completed to the date of
termination. Designer may terminate on 30 days' written notice.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Designer shall keep secret all commercial information disclosed by Client during
the engagement and shall not disclose it without consent. This obligation
continues after termination of this agreement for 2 years.

7. LIABILITY

Designer shall be liable to Client for any loss arising from Designer's failure
to perform the services, including any lost sales or damage to Client's
business or reputation resulting from the Deliverables.

8. GENERAL

This agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland. Each party shall act in good
faith. If any clause is unenforceable the remainder stands.
