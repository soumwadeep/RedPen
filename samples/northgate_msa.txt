MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made between Northgate Digital Ltd ("Client")
and the individual identified in the Order Form ("Contractor").

1. ENGAGEMENT AND AVAILABILITY

Contractor shall be available for Client's projects on a full-time basis for the
duration of each Order Form and shall work from Client's office three days per
week during Client's standard business hours of 9:00 to 18:00. Contractor shall
follow Client's internal policies, including its security handbook and reporting
structure, and shall report daily to Client's project manager. Contractor shall
not engage in any other client work or assignment during the term of this
Agreement and for six months after its termination, and acknowledges that other
clients in the same industry are competitors of Client.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

Client shall pay Contractor the fixed fee set out in the Order Form. All
invoices shall be paid within 90 days of Client's approval of the relevant
Deliverable, and payment shall be made only when the Client receives the
corresponding funds from its end client. Payment of each invoice is subject to
the Client's complete satisfaction with the work performed. Client may withhold
or set off any amount it considers necessary against any claim it may have.
Fees are inclusive of all taxes, including any withholding, sales tax, GST or
VAT, which shall be Contractor's sole responsibility. No deposit or advance
payment shall be made.

3. REVISIONS AND SCOPE

Contractor shall provide unlimited revisions of each Deliverable until the
Client is satisfied. Client may modify, add to or change the scope,
requirements and Deliverables at any time at its sole discretion, and
Contractor shall perform such additional requests as reasonably required within
the agreed fee and timeline.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All work product, including all drafts, source files, layered project files,
concepts, sketches and derivative works, shall be the exclusive property of the
Client upon creation, whether or not payment has been made. Contractor hereby
assigns all right, title and interest in such work product to the Client, and
waives all moral rights in respect of it to the fullest extent permitted by
law. Contractor shall not display, publish or reference any Deliverable in its
portfolio, website, case studies or award entries at any time. Client may
revoke any licence granted to Contractor at any time.

5. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

Contractor shall indemnify, defend and hold the Client harmless from and
against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses
howsoever arising, without limitation, including indirect, incidental, special
and consequential damages and lost profits, regardless of the cause of such
claim. Nothing in this Agreement shall limit the Contractor's liability, which
is unlimited. Contractor shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not
less than 5,000,000 USD and provide evidence of insurance before starting work.

6. DELAY AND PERFORMANCE

Time is of the essence. Where any Deliverable is late, Contractor shall pay
liquidated damages of 250 USD per day of delay for each and every day until
delivery. Client may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form at any time for
convenience without liability for any work in progress, and Contractor shall
receive no further payment on termination.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Contractor shall keep confidential all information, materials, data and know-
how learned, observed or obtained during the engagement, without limitation,
including Contractor's general skills and techniques. The confidentiality
obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

8. TERM AND RENEWAL

This Agreement begins on the Order Form date for an initial term of 6 months and
shall automatically renew for successive 6 month periods unless Contractor
gives 7 days' prior written notice of termination. Client may assign this
Agreement or its rights hereunder freely.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delaware, and the parties submit to
the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Wilmington, Delaware. Any dispute
shall be resolved by binding arbitration and Contractor waives any right to
participate in a class action.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No
acceptance criteria, schedule of deliverables or specification is incorporated
unless issued by Client in writing at Client's sole discretion.
